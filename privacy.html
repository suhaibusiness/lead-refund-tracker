Privacy

Last updated 3 November 2026. In short: the lead emails and customer details you paste stay in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and you send every claim yourself.

What stays in your browser

  • Your leads, the emails and CSV files you paste or open, your outcomes, claims and settings are saved in this browser’s local storage (keys lead-ledger-v1 and ll-theme), so the tool remembers them next time.
  • Emails and CSV files are read in your browser. They are never sent anywhere.
  • Claim text is written in your browser from fixed templates. The tool never contacts Angi, Thumbtack, Google, Bark, hipages, Yelp or anyone else for you: you copy the text and send it yourself.
  • The export file is saved to your own computer.

What leaves your browser

  • Nothing you enter. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies.
  • This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives the normal request details when you load a page, and GitHub says it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Links to each platform’s rules open that platform’s own help pages only when you click them.

Your customers’ details

Lead emails hold your customers’ names and phone numbers. They stay on your device, and you can remove them at any time: Settings → Delete everything, or clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

Not legal advice

Lead Refund Tracker summarises each platform’s own written credit and refund rules, with the date we read them. It is not legal advice. The platforms change their rules, and their own terms decide every claim.

Who runs this

Lead Refund Tracker is a free beta made by Suhaib Akmal. It is independent and not connected to any of the lead platforms it mentions.
