Angi lead credits

How to get a lead credit from Angi, and which bad leads qualify

Request it on the lead within 45 days, and only for a reason on Angi’s list. Angi reviews it within 5 business days. An approved credit comes off your future lead charges and expires if you don’t use it within six months.

The five reasons that qualify

  • Invalid contact info. The phone number is disconnected, not in service, or isn’t the homeowner’s.
  • Wrong location. The project is outside the ZIP codes set in your profile.
  • Service mismatch. The project type doesn’t match the services or tasks in your profile.
  • Duplicate lead. You were charged twice for the same lead or homeowner within 45 days.
  • Paused leads. You were charged for a lead while your lead flow was paused.

Angi says it may also offer credits for exceptional cases that aren’t on this list.

The conditions

  • The lead is less than 45 days old.
  • You tried to call the homeowner within 24 hours of getting the lead.
  • Your account is in good standing, with no overdue balance.
  • You don’t have an annual subscription.

On an annual subscription you can still report a lead, but Angi offers no credit for it. If you have a concern about a lead, Angi’s Customer Care number is (877) 947-3639.

“The lead must be less than 45 days old.”

What never qualifies

  • The homeowner doesn’t answer your calls or return your messages.
  • They hired another pro before you reached them.
  • They decided not to go ahead for now.
  • They’re collecting several quotes (the “shopping phase”).
  • You quoted but didn’t win the job.

Angi says it already allows for these in what a lead costs.

“The homeowner does not answer your calls or return your messages.”

Angi Help Center, on what isn’t eligible, read 5 November 2026

How to ask

  • In the Angi for Pros app: open the Leads tab and the lead, tap the menu in the upper right corner, then Request Credit. Read the requirements, tap Continue, pick the reason, add details and tap Continue.
  • On the web: sign in to your business account, open Leads, select the lead and choose Request lead credit near the top. Click Continue, choose the reason and click Continue again.

Angi reviews each request within 5 business days. Approved credits apply to your next lead charges by themselves.

“…credits will expire if not used within six months from the date issued…”

Example

Details for a disconnected number (change them to yours):

Reason: Invalid contact info

I called the number on this lead at 9:05am on 4 Nov, within an hour of getting it, and again at 1:30pm. Both times I got “this number is not in service”.

The catch. “Fake lead” isn’t a reason on Angi’s list, so a claim worded that way has nothing to match. Pick the reason that fits: invalid contact info for a bad number, wrong location or service mismatch for the wrong job. And a homeowner who simply goes quiet gets no credit, however many times you call.
