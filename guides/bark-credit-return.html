Bark credit returns

How to get your credits back from Bark for a bad lead

Claim within 14 days of buying the lead, online, for a reason on Bark’s list. Bark returns credits to your account, not money, and aims to answer within 48 hours. Every return is at Bark’s discretion.

The time limit

  • Ask within 14 days of buying the lead. Later requests are declined.
  • Ask online only, through Bark’s Credit Return Request Form. Elite Pro members use Request Return in My Responses.
  • Bark aims to answer within 48 hours. What comes back is credits for future leads, not money.

“…You can request a Credit Return within 14 days of purchase…”

The reasons that qualify

  • Invalid phone number: unreachable, disconnected, or someone else’s.
  • Invalid email address: it bounces or doesn’t exist.
  • Wrong service: a request made in error, not meant, or without the customer’s consent, including minors and people after free work.
  • Incorrect information: wrong category, wrong location or a scope that wasn’t true.
  • Platform misuse: phishing, bots or a competitor testing.
  • Job seeker: the person wanted a job, not a service.
  • Inappropriate behaviour: the customer was abusive or threatening.
  • Accidental purchase: a one-off mistake on an account in good standing.
  • Duplicate charge: you paid for the same lead twice.

What never qualifies

  • The customer hired someone else.
  • The customer didn’t answer your calls or messages.
  • The customer changed the job after you spoke.
  • Leads outside your area or trade that your lead settings should have filtered out.
  • More than one “accidental” purchase.

To skip a lead without paying, use Not interested on it: Bark tells the customer for you.

“…A customer not answering or replying doesn't qualify as an invalid lead…”

The evidence Bark checks

  • JPG, PNG or PDF only.
  • Screenshots from an email app, messaging app or CRM, clear and unedited.
  • The contact details in the screenshot match the lead.
  • The whole conversation, with timestamps.
  • Send it from your Bark account email.

Bark also says many claims with the same or doubtful evidence can pause reviews on your account.

Your first pack: the Get Hired Guarantee

  • It covers your first Starter Pack or first app purchase, and offers that say they include it.
  • You qualify once you’ve used your credits (or can’t afford another lead), 3 calendar days have passed since your last lead, and you weren’t marked as hired.
  • You get back the credits you used, not the whole pack, once per purchase. Contact Bark support to claim.

Cash refunds are rare: an unused credit pack, or a Starter Pack that got no leads in its first month. Bark says local consumer law applies, so name your country in the request.

“The credits you used not the original pack size.”

Example

A claim for a dead number (change the details to yours):

Reason: Invalid phone number

Bought this lead on 4 Nov at 9:10am. Called at 9:15am and 2:40pm: both times "the number you have called is not connected". Screenshots attached of my call log with times, from my account email.

The catch. Most money is lost to the 14-day clock, not the rules: a lead that goes quiet on day 3 is found to be a dead number on day 20, and by then it can’t be claimed. Note the date you bought each lead and test the number the same day.
