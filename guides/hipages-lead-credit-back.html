How to get hipages credits back for a bad lead

Report it in the hipages for business app within 7 days of accepting the lead, for a reason on hipages’ list. If hipages agrees, the lead’s value goes back on your credit balance, not your bank account. Anything not on the list counts as a genuine lead.

The time limit

  • Report within 7 days of accepting the lead. After that the report option is gone.
  • hipages says it reviews the lead and tells you the outcome within 5 business days.
  • Reporting a lead marks the job as cancelled. You can change the status back by hand.

“…At any time within 7 days after accepting a lead…”

The reasons on hipages’ list

  • Job not as described: the job needs work the lead card didn’t mention.
  • Wrong trade category: it isn’t your trade, or it is but needs a skill you don’t offer (hipages’ example: test and tag for an electrician).
  • Outside your service area: the job isn’t in the area you set, or isn’t where the lead said.
  • Invalid phone number and email address: both, not one (see the day-5 rule below).
  • Other: anything else, explained in your own words.

hipages says leads that don’t meet these criteria are genuine leads. A customer who simply doesn’t reply isn’t on the list.

Wrong contact details: the day-5 rule

  • Choose “Contact details are wrong” only if both the phone number and the email are wrong.
  • The customer must also have had 5 days to answer you in the Message Centre. The app won’t let you report this before day 5.
  • So the window for this reason is short: days 5 to 7 after you accept the lead.
  • Say what you tried: the calls, the messages, and which details were wrong.

“…Only use this option if both the phone number and email address are incorrect…”

The location limit

  • If the job is somewhere other than the lead said, choose “Job not in this location” and say where it really is.
  • If it’s in a postcode you don’t want, “I don’t service this location” works only twice. After that, a lead inside your set area is refused.
  • So turn off the postcodes you don’t serve in your coverage settings, rather than reporting the same problem again.

How to report a lead in the app

  • In hipages for business, open the job and go to its Job Diary.
  • On the “Lead accepted” record, tap the report icon.
  • Pick the reason and explain it in detail: what was wrong, what the job really is, what you tried.

If the report is valid, the value of the lead comes back as credits. Returned credits follow the same expiry as the credits you paid for.

“…hipages will credit the value of the lead back to your lead credit balance…”

Example

A report for a job that wasn’t as described (change it to yours):

Reason: Job is not as described

Accepted 4 Nov. The lead said “replace 2 power points”. On the call the customer wants a full switchboard upgrade and rewiring of the kitchen, which the lead card didn’t mention and I don’t quote on.

The catch. The 7 days run from the moment you accept, and the contact-details reason can’t be used until day 5. A lead that goes quiet is easy to forget until day 8. Write down the date you accept each lead and check on day 5.
