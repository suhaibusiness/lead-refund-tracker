Thumbtack lead refunds

Thumbtack refund for a bad lead: what qualifies, and the one that’s automatic

Ask within 45 days of the charge, with screenshots. Thumbtack usually answers in 24 to 48 hours, or up to four days if it needs a closer look. If the customer simply never replied, you may not need to ask at all: Fair Shot refunds that lead by itself when four conditions hold.

“…you can request a refund within 45 days of the charge…”

Thumbtack Help: Refund policy for pros, read 5 November 2026

What usually qualifies

  • Invalid phone number. The number is disconnected or invalid. Say why you think so.
  • Duplicate lead or double charge. The same customer, for the same job, within 45 days. Two different jobs from one customer aren’t a duplicate.
  • The job changed a lot. For example, the customer moved it outside your travel area. Say which details changed.
  • The lead breaks Thumbtack’s Terms of Use. Report the customer to request the refund.
  • A fixed date you’d blocked off. A wedding or a move on a date that can’t change, which you’d already blocked in your calendar.
  • A first message of “no thanks”, but only from customers you reached through the Opportunities tab.

Thumbtack decides each case on its circumstances, and its page says every refund is at its sole discretion.

The customer never replied: Fair Shot

This is the complaint owners raise most, and it’s the one case with an automatic refund. Thumbtack refunds the lead without a request when all four of these hold:

  • Your first contact went through Thumbtack: its Messenger or a Thumbtack number.
  • The customer replied to no pro, you or anyone else, within 72 hours of sending the request.
  • You accepted the lead and replied within four business hours (8am to 8pm, your local time).
  • You didn’t share your own phone number or email before the customer’s first reply.

A call from the customer counts as a reply, even one you miss. The refund goes to your Thumbtack balance and shows in Payments.

“…if a customer doesn’t reply to any pro they contacted within 72 hours…”

Thumbtack Help: Fair Shot Refund policy, read 5 November 2026

What doesn’t qualify

  • The customer hired a different pro.
  • A free lead, because you weren’t charged.
  • Leads you got because your preferences or account settings weren’t set up for the jobs you want. Tighten your service area, job types and availability instead.
  • A request sent more than 45 days after the charge, or one without enough evidence.
  • A lead with a flexible date. Suggest other dates to the customer first; if that goes nowhere, Thumbtack says to request a refund and it will look into it.

How to ask, and what to send

  1. Request the refund within 45 days of the charge.
  2. Name the reason in Thumbtack’s own words: invalid phone number, duplicate lead, or the job changed significantly.
  3. Add the detail: what you tried, when, and what happened. Attach up to six screenshots.
  4. Follow it in the app under Profile, the gear icon, Payments, then View your balance and charges. Each request shows Pending, Refunded, Credited or Denied.
  5. Want the money on your card instead of your Thumbtack balance? Contact Thumbtack within 10 business days of the approval. Credit packages and promotional balances only go back to your balance.

Example

A request for a disconnected number (change the details to yours):

Reason: The customer provided an invalid phone number

I called (555) 010-0199 at 9:12am and 2:40pm on 4 Nov and got “number not in service” both times. I also messaged through Thumbtack at 9:10am and had no reply. Screenshots of my call log and the message are attached.

The catch. Most refunds are Thumbtack’s judgement call, not a right. And the customer who never answers is covered only by Fair Shot, so reply through Thumbtack within four business hours and keep your own number out of the first message, or that lead can’t be refunded.
